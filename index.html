Hourglassy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Hourglassy respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the Hourglassy Android application.

1. Accounts

Hourglassy does not require users to create an account or sign in. Hourglassy does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, or other account information in order to use the app.

2. Advertising

The free version of Hourglassy may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions
  • Diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance
  • Device or other identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

Users who purchase Hourglassy Pro may receive an ad-free experience as described within the app.

3. Hourglassy Pro and Purchases

Hourglassy may offer a one-time purchase for Hourglassy Pro through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google Play. Hourglassy does not receive or store your credit card number, bank account details, or other payment credentials.

To verify a purchase and provide Pro access, Hourglassy may process information related to the purchase, including the Google Play product identifier and purchase token. This information may be transmitted securely to a purchase-verification service for the purposes of confirming entitlement, providing app functionality, and preventing fraud.

4. Local Files and User Content

Hourglassy may allow you to select local content such as sounds, backgrounds, or backup files. These files are intended to be processed locally on your device. Hourglassy does not upload these user-selected files to its servers.

If you explicitly export or share a file using Android system features, the destination and handling of that file are controlled by you and the selected Android application or service.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Hourglassy or its service providers may be used for:

  • Providing and maintaining app functionality
  • Verifying Hourglassy Pro purchases
  • Displaying and measuring advertising
  • Analytics
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance

6. Third-Party Services

Hourglassy uses services provided by Google, including Google Play and Google Mobile Ads. These services may process information according to Google's own privacy practices and policies.

Hourglassy may also communicate with a secure purchase-verification service to validate Google Play purchases.

7. Data Security

Data transmitted by Hourglassy and supported third-party services is intended to be transmitted using encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

8. Data Stored on Your Device

Hourglassy stores app settings, timers, configurations, and related information locally on your device as necessary for the app to function. Removing the app or clearing its application data may remove locally stored Hourglassy data.

9. Children

Hourglassy is not specifically designed for or directed to children under 13 years of age.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Hourglassy's features, services, or privacy practices change. The current version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
