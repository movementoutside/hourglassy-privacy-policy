Hourglassy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Hourglassy respects your privacy. This policy describes how information may be accessed, collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Hourglassy Android application. The practices that apply can depend on the installed app version, your choices, and your region.

1. Accounts

Hourglassy does not require an account or sign-in. You do not need to give Hourglassy your name, email address, or phone number to use its timers.

2. Advertising and Privacy Choices

The free version of Hourglassy may display ads through Google Mobile Ads, including native ads and, where offered, optional rewarded ads for background customization. Hourglassy Pro does not display ads. Ads and privacy messages may differ across app versions and regions.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) helps the app present consent or privacy choices where applicable. A privacy choice may affect whether advertising can be requested. Where the feature is available, you can revisit applicable privacy options from the app's Settings. Declining personalized advertising is not necessarily the same as disabling all contextual or limited ads.

Google's advertising and consent services may process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location
  • Interactions with the app and ads, including launches, taps, and ad views
  • Diagnostics and performance information
  • Device or other identifiers, including advertising or app-set identifiers where available

Depending on the applicable service and settings, this information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Choosing non-personalized ads does not necessarily stop all data processing. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

3. Age-Based Advertising Safeguards

Hourglassy is intended for people aged 13 and older. A closed-test version 6 was submitted without a neutral age selection screen. App versions without this screen do not apply the age-selection safeguards described below.

In app versions that include the optional age selector, you may choose Under 13, 13–15, 16–17, 18 or older, or Prefer not to say. The choice is stored on your device without requesting your exact birth date. You can change it from Settings, and the timer remains usable if you decline to answer. Hourglassy does not infer your age from your Google account or a purchase.

In those age-selector versions, advertising is disabled for people who select Under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or Prefer not to say, and when no age category is available. Only eligible free-version users who select 18 or older may receive ads, subject to applicable privacy choices. These rules describe the age-selector implementation and do not claim that older versions already contain it.

4. Hourglassy Pro and Purchases

Hourglassy may offer a one-time Pro purchase through Google Play. Google Play processes payments; Hourglassy does not receive or store your card number, bank account details, or other payment credentials.

To validate a purchase, restore an entitlement, and help prevent fraud, Hourglassy may process the purchased product identifier, purchase token, and related purchase-verification information. Such information may be sent to a purchase-verification service. Google Play also processes information under its own privacy practices. A Pro user may still have a privacy-status refresh through UMP in app versions that support this, without loading ads.

5. Local Files and User Content

If Hourglassy lets you select sounds, backgrounds, or backup files, these are intended to be processed locally. Hourglassy does not intentionally upload user-selected files to its own servers. When you choose to export or share files using Android's system features, handling by the destination app or service is controlled by that destination and your choices.

6. How Information Is Used

Depending on the feature and app version, information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain timers, settings, and app functionality
  • Verify Pro purchases and restore purchased features
  • Deliver and measure eligible advertising
  • Diagnose technical or service problems
  • Support security, fraud prevention, and applicable compliance obligations

7. Third-Party Services

Hourglassy uses Google services, including Google Play, Google Mobile Ads, and UMP. Their data processing may include service providers or other recipients involved in advertising and service delivery, subject to their applicable terms and settings. A purchase-verification service may also process purchase-related information. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's practices.

8. Data Security

Hourglassy uses or relies on secure connections, such as HTTPS/TLS, when transmitting information through its supported network integrations. Third-party services are responsible for the security of their own processing. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

9. Local Storage, Retention and Deletion

Hourglassy stores timers, settings, and related app configuration locally on your device. In versions with an age selector, the selected age category is also stored locally. Local data generally remains until you remove it through available app controls, clear Hourglassy's app storage, or uninstall the app. Android backup or other services, if enabled, may handle copies under their own settings and policies.

Purchase verification records and information processed by third-party services may be retained for entitlement verification, security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or other purposes described by those providers. Uninstalling Hourglassy or clearing local data does not necessarily erase information already held by Google or a verification provider. Retention of such information is subject to the relevant service and applicable law.

10. Requests and Control Over Your Data

You can change available advertising and age choices in Settings in app versions that support them. You can remove locally stored Hourglassy data using Android's app-storage controls or by uninstalling the app, subject to any enabled backups. You can send privacy questions or requests concerning data processed for Hourglassy to vibricsolutions@gmail.com. Requests will be assessed according to applicable law and the information under the developer's control. For data controlled independently by Google, use Google's privacy controls or its published contact procedures.

Hourglassy does not provide user accounts, so there is no Hourglassy account to delete. A privacy request does not guarantee deletion of information that another provider must retain for legitimate purposes.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may change when Hourglassy's features, service providers, or privacy practices change. The current policy will appear on this page, with an updated date when revised.
